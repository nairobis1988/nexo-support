Privacy Policy for Nexo Social

Effective date: May 23, 2026

Nexo Social (“Nexo”, “we”, “our”, or “us”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect information when users access or use the Nexo Social mobile application and related services.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect information needed to operate the app, including account information, profile details, email address, username, display name, uploaded content, comments, messages, device information, notification tokens, app activity, crash logs, and usage analytics.

2. User Content

Nexo allows users to create, upload, share, and interact with content such as photos, videos, reels, stories, comments, profile information, and community posts. Users are responsible for the content they upload and share through the app.

3. Camera, Microphone, Photos and Storage

Nexo may request access to the camera, microphone, photo library, media storage, and device files so users can create videos, reels, stories, upload photos, record audio, send media, and use content creation features. These permissions are used only to provide app functionality requested by the user.

4. Notifications

We may collect and use push notification tokens to send notifications related to comments, messages, interactions, updates, account activity, safety alerts, and other app features.

5. Location Data

If location-based features are enabled, Nexo may request access to location data to provide relevant app functionality. Users can control location permissions from their device settings.

6. Firebase and Third-Party Services

Nexo may use Firebase and other trusted service providers for authentication, cloud storage, database services, push notifications, analytics, crash reporting, security, and app performance. These services help us operate and improve the app.

7. Advertising and Analytics

Nexo may use analytics and advertising services to understand app usage, improve performance, measure engagement, and support app monetization. These services may collect device identifiers, app activity, and diagnostic information according to their own privacy practices.

8. How We Use Information

  • To create and manage user accounts.
  • To provide content sharing, messaging, comments, videos, reels, and community features.
  • To store and display user-generated content.
  • To send notifications and app updates.
  • To improve app performance, safety, security, and user experience.
  • To prevent abuse, spam, fraud, or policy violations.
  • To comply with legal, safety, and platform requirements.

9. Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared with service providers that help us operate the app, comply with legal obligations, protect users, enforce policies, or provide features requested by users. Public content shared by users may be visible to other users of the app.

10. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to help protect user information. However, no online service can guarantee complete security. Users should protect their account credentials and report any suspicious activity.

11. Data Retention and Account Deletion

We retain information as needed to provide the app, maintain security, comply with legal obligations, and resolve disputes. Users may request account deletion or privacy assistance by contacting us at nexosocialsupport@gmail.com.

12. Children’s Privacy

Nexo is not intended for children under the minimum age required by applicable law. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children where parental consent is required.

13. User Controls

Users can manage certain privacy controls through the app and device settings, including notification permissions, camera access, microphone access, media access, and location permissions.

14. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

15. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, privacy practices, support, account deletion, or user data, contact us at:
nexosocialsupport@gmail.com
